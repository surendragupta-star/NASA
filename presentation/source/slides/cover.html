NASA Space Apps Challenge · Astronaut health

Crew Health Console

Self-assessment software that helps astronauts spot health changes on long missions and know when to act.

Team SURENDRA · Demo: surendragupta-star.github.io/NASA

Hi, we are team SURENDRA. This is Crew Health Console, our answer to the challenge “Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions.” It helps astronauts check their own health during long missions, understand what the results mean in space, and decide whether to handle a problem themselves or call a flight surgeon.
