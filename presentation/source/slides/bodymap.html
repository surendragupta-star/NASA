Crew Health Console · Sample mission day 23

Here is the heart of the app: a body map. Each region is colored by its worst current reading, symptom or trend. In this sample on mission day 23, the head is red because the astronaut slept only 5.6 hours, the heart is amber because blood pressure is creeping up, and the legs are amber because exercise has dropped. Tapping a region explains what that system does, how spaceflight affects it, what to do, and when to call a flight surgeon. Tiles and 21-day charts show each indicator, and trend checks catch slow problems that one reading would miss.
