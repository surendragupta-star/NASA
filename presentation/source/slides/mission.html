Crew Health Console · Environment settings

The console knows where the crew is. Pick a space station, deep-space transit, a lunar base, a rover or a Mars base, and it adjusts the expected radiation dose, gravity, bone-loss estimate and signal delay. The escalation advice changes too. Near Earth, the crew sends the report and follows the ground. On Mars, where replies take up to 48 minutes, the console tells the crew to start the onboard medical checklist right away. Radiation rates come from NASA research and from measurements by the Curiosity rover and the Chang’e 4 lander.
