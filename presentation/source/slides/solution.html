Crew Health Console · How it works

Crew Health Console answers three questions. How am I doing? Astronauts log 11 indicators that standard onboard equipment can measure, from heart rate and blood pressure to sleep, mood, radiation dose and body mass, plus any symptoms. Why does it matter up here? Every field and body system has plain-language explanations. Should I act or call the ground? Each reading is graded Nominal, Watch or Act now, and every flag comes with a specific next step.
